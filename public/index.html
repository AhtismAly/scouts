Welcome

Scouting rooted in Hunza, part of a world movement

The Ismaili District Boy Scouts Association Hunza is the Scouting programme of the Ismaili Jamat of Hunza, a branch of the Gilgit-Baltistan Boy Scouts Association and part of the Pakistan Boy Scouts Association. We stand with Ismaili Scouts across Pakistan and around the world, following the method Robert Baden-Powell set out in 1907: learning by doing, in small patrols, outdoors, guided by the ethic of service at the heart of the Jamat.

From the Shaheen Unit for the youngest members, through the Scout Unit, to Rovers and adult leaders, every stage builds practical skills, self-reliance and a habit of looking after others. Our uniformed members serve at community gatherings, run relief and clean-up drives, and take to the mountains of Gilgit-Baltistan, Chitral and beyond for camps that stay with them for life.
