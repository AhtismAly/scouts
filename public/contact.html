Join us

Scouting is open to young people aged 5 to 25 and to adults who want to volunteer as leaders, trainers or supporters.

Sections

Find your unit

Shaheen Unit (ages 5 to 10)

The youngest section: games, stories, first badges and the first taste of the outdoors.

Scout Unit (ages 11 to 17)

Patrols, camps, pioneering, first aid and community service. The heart of the programme.

Rovers (ages 18 to 25)

Expeditions, leadership and service projects for older Scouts stepping into adult life.

Adult leaders & volunteers

No Scouting background needed; training is provided. Bring your skills and your time.

Ismaili Scouts

Ismaili Scouts in Pakistan, registered with the Pakistan Boy Scouts Association. Building character, skills and a spirit of service in young people, in the tradition begun by our first Chief Scout, Prince Aly Khan, in 1930.
